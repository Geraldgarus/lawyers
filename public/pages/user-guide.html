The Core Idea: Everything Lives Inside a Case

A Case is the hub for one legal matter. Hearings, documents, tasks, expenses, invoices, and notes are all created from inside that case — never anywhere else. The Calendar, Documents, Tasks, Expenses, and Billing menus are just views that pull everything together across all cases so you can search and browse — they are not separate places you enter new data.

Registering a Client & Opening a Case

  1. Go to Cases and click + New Case.
  2. Search for the client by name. If they're new, click Add New Client Instead and fill in their details right there — no need to visit the Clients page first.
  3. Fill in the case title, type, and any known details, then click Create Case.
  4. You're taken straight to the new case's page, where everything else happens.
If the same client returns later for a different matter, search for their existing name instead of adding them again — this keeps their full case history in one place under Clients.

Scheduling a Hearing

  1. Open the case, go to the Hearings tab, click Add Hearing.
  2. It automatically appears on the Calendar and on the Dashboard as the date approaches.
  3. After the hearing happens, come back and click the clipboard icon to record the outcome (e.g. "Adjourned to 15 Aug"), then add the next hearing date if there is one.

Billing a Client

  1. Open the case, go to the Payments tab, click New Invoice.
  2. When the client pays (in full or partially), click the money icon next to the invoice to Record Payment.
  3. The outstanding balance updates automatically and shows on the case Overview.
Billing & Payments is only visible to the Lawyer role.

The Timeline Tab

The Timeline tab on every case is automatically generated — every hearing, document, completed task, expense, payment, and note you add shows up there in order, with no extra effort. Use the Notes tab instead for private thoughts or meeting summaries that don't belong anywhere else.

Roles

  • Lawyer — full access, including billing, reports, and user management.
  • Secretary — can manage clients, cases, hearings, documents, tasks, and expenses, but cannot see billing figures or reports.
  • Assistant — can manage clients, hearings, documents, and tasks, but cannot edit cases, expenses, or see billing.

See the full breakdown on the Permissions & Access page.
